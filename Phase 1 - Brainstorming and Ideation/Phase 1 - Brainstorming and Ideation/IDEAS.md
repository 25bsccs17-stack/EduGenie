# Brainstorming and Ideation

## Project Name
EduGenie – Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

## Problem Identified

Students often face difficulty understanding complex topics, preparing for examinations, creating quizzes, and planning their study time effectively.

## Initial Ideas

### Idea 1: AI Learning Assistant
Create an AI assistant that explains educational topics in simple and easy-to-understand language.

### Idea 2: Exam Preparation Assistant
Provide exam-focused explanations, important points, and different answer formats for students.

### Idea 3: Quiz Generator
Generate multiple-choice questions automatically based on the topic entered by the student.
